About Us

Building the Future of Work

We're on a mission to make AI automation accessible, practical, and transformative for every business.

MunibaVo was born from a simple insight: most businesses waste thousands of hours every year on tasks that AI can handle better, faster, and cheaper.

Founded by a team of AI engineers and business strategists in Ahmedabad, India, we bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI and practical business applications.

Today, we've built over 500 automation systems for companies across industries — from scrappy startups to enterprise organizations in 15+ countries.

"We don't just build bots — we engineer intelligent systems that think, learn, and evolve with your business."
